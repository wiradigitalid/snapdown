# Icon provenance and licence

Every SVG in this folder except `blur.svg` and `marker.svg` comes from [Lucide](https://lucide.dev),
or is the [Feather](https://feathericons.com) original that Lucide inherited (`hard-drive.svg` is
Feather's `save`). Some carry a different name here than upstream (`message-square.svg` is Lucide's
`message-circle`), and several are the upstream geometry serialised differently; the shapes are
Lucide's either way. `blur.svg` and `marker.svg` are drawn for Snapdown and fall under the
repository's own MIT licence.

The ISC licence below asks that this notice appear in every copy. It does: here, beside the files,
and on the About tab of the Settings screen inside the application (`NTL-3` in
`.control/project-non-technical-log.md`). An icon added from any other set MUST be recorded here
with its own notice.
