**Read 32% as a floor, not a measurement.** Three things distort it, and each already has a record.

**The registry cannot see four shipped waves.** `CAP-1`, `CAP-4`, `CAP-5`, `CAP-7` and `CAP-8` show
`not-started` in the timeline above, and all five are in the field in `r1`/`r2`. The cause is `OQ-21`:
W2–W5 closed without their story lists ever reaching `waves.yaml`, and `timeline.py` derives its
dates from story-file history. Fourteen story files exist on disk and not one carries a `UC-`
reference, so the link cannot be reconstructed without inventing it — which `OQ-21` weighed and
declined. **The number understates what shipped, and it will keep doing so.**

**A green row can be green against a promise that has since changed.** `FR-5` reads green on
`W1-S3`'s tests. `DEC-004` rewrote `FR-5` — the Quality Budget is now a named intent with `Auto`
deriving per capture — and none of that is built; `W6-S4` carries it. The RTM has no way to know a
requirement was rewritten under a passing test. Worth remembering before quoting any single row.

**Every date in this report is 2026-08-23**, so the gantt has no time dimension worth reading. The
whole history is one day.

### What the 32% is measuring honestly

Ten RTM rows turned green, and six of them turned green **today**: `FR-27` and `FR-28` from `W6-S2`,
and `FR-6` through `FR-9` from `W6-S7`. Those six are the wave working.

`FR-6` through `FR-9` matter more than the count suggests: before `W6-S7`, `MarkerLayer` was mounted
nowhere and the Editor never rendered a Finding's image at all (`BUG-5`). `FR-8` — place numbered
Markers bound to numbered Note lines — is the promise `AD-1` and `BG-1` are both built on, and it had
no user interface. It has one now.

### The defect register is where the real state is

Eleven registered, and the ratio is the fact worth keeping: **not one is a wrong requirement.** Every
one is code that disagrees with a requirement already `active`, or a seam nobody owned. Section 4
above groups by root cause and finds no `requirement` bucket at all.

Three are closed or effectively closed. `BUG-5` is deliberately marked
`resolved-pending-product-verification` rather than `resolved`: jsdom does not fetch images and
`convertFileSrc` is mocked, so its tests pass whether or not a real image loads.

**`BUG-11` gates the ones still open.** There is no reproducible way to build this application, so
`BUG-4` and `BUG-5` cannot be proven in a running product — only in jsdom. `W6-S11` is placed fourth
in the wave for that reason rather than for its own severity.
